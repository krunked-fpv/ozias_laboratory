## 🌟 What Is This Repo?

Welcome to **`ozias_laboratory`**—a custom-built, Pokémon-inspired math RPG and interactive training arcade engineered for **Ozias** (and his co-op tag-team partner, **Mom Marisa**)!

Because Ozias is a 3rd grader who loves math and is already excelling well beyond standard classroom worksheets, this app features a live **Multi-Grade League Difficulty Dial** right in the top bar and Command Center. With one click, Ozias or Marisa can dial the math engine from **3rd Grade Core (Lv.30)** all the way up through **4th Grade Boost (Lv.40)**, **5th Grade Challenger (Lv.50)**, **6th Grade Master League (Lv.60+)**, or turn on **⚡ Adaptive Evolution Mode** so the questions automatically scale up as his winning streak grows!

## 🎚️ The 5-Mode Grade Level Difficulty Dial

| League Tier | Level | Core Math Skills Unlocked | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| 🪨 **Boulder League (3rd Grade Core)** | **Lv. 30** | Multiplication & division facts ($0\text{–}10$), 3-digit addition & subtraction with regrouping, unit fractions, and area/perimeter of rectangles. | 
| 🌊 **Cascade League (4th Grade Boost)** | **Lv. 40** | Full $12 \times 12$ fact mastery, 2-digit $\times$ 1-digit multiplication, division, equivalent fractions, 4-digit operations, and 1-step $x$-variable puzzles. | 
| ⚡ **Thunder League (5th Grade Challenger)** | **Lv. 50** | 2-digit $\times$ 2-digit partial products, long division, percentages ($10\%, 25\%, 50\%, 75\%$), order of operations (PEMDAS), and 3D prism volume. | 
| 🐉 **Dragon League (6th Grade Master)** | **Lv. 60+** | Exponents ($2^5, 4^3, 11^2$), negative integer operations, ratios & proportions, and 2-step algebraic equations ($4x + 20 = 100$). | 
| 🌟 **Adaptive Auto-Dial Mode** | **⚡ AUTO** | Starts at 3rd Grade Core and automatically levels up to 4th, 5th, and 6th Grade problems as Ozias builds a $3+, 6+,$ and $9+$ streak! | 

## 🕹️ Inside the 6 PokéMath League Arenas

### 🎛️ Arena 0: League HQ, Grade Dial & Starter Partner Evolution

* **4 Starter Partner Lines (3 Evolution Stages Each):** Pick **Voltchu** ($\rightarrow$ *Thunderpounce* $\rightarrow$ *Stormlux Rex*), **Pyrokit** ($\rightarrow$ *Blazefang* $\rightarrow$ *Infernodrake*), **Aquafin** ($\rightarrow$ *Torrentshell* $\rightarrow$ *Hydroleviathan*), or **Leafsprout** ($\rightarrow$ *Verdantclaw* $\rightarrow$ *Terradon Prime*).

* **👩‍👦 Ozias & Mom (Marisa) Tag-Team Mode:** Toggle between **Solo Trainer Mode** and **Pass & Play Co-Op Mode** where Ozias and Mom alternate turns launching attacks and racking up team points!

* **⚡ Elemental Power Surge Orbs:** 4 instant mental-math warmup cards across Electric ($\times/\div$), Fire ($+/-$), Water (Fractions/$\%$), and Psychic (Algebra/Geometry).

### ⚔️ Arena 1: Wild Creature & Gym Boss Battle Arena

* **Turn-Based RPG Math Combat:** Battle wild elemental creatures and summon **Epic Gym Leader Bosses**! Answering in under 8 seconds lands a **CRITICAL HIT (**$1.5\times$ **Damage + Bonus PokéCoins)**.

* **Choose Your Attack Move Type:** Filter your attacks by **⚡ Bolt Strike (**$\times/\div$**)**, **🔥 Flame Charge (**$+/-$ **& Place Value)**, **🌊 Hydro Pulse (Fractions & Percents)**, **🔮 Psychic Beam (Algebra & Geometry)**, or **🌟 All Moves**.

* **🔍 Poké-Scanner Visual Strategy Hints:** Reveals non-spoiler mental-math strategies (Area Splits, Fact Families, PEMDAS rules, or Algebra Balance Scales) without breaking your flow.

* **✏️ Built-In Digital Scratchpad Canvas:** Work out multi-step calculations right beside the battle screen using a mouse, finger, or tablet stylus!

### 🔴 Arena 2: Poké-Ball Speed Catch & Interactive $12 \times 12$ Power Matrix

* **Mode 1 — Speed-Catch Blitz:** Run a **60s Quick Ball**, **90s Great Ball**, **3-Minute Ultra Ball**, or **Untimed Zen Mode** sprint to build your high score.

* **Mode 2 — Interactive** $12 \times 12$ **Power Matrix:** Click any cell on the 144-fact board (or hit *Challenge* $\times 6, \times 7, \times 8, \times 9$) to solve fact families and light up both commutative twins in gold!

### 🧪 Arena 3: Professor's Lab — Mega-Split, Fraction Reactor & Story Quests

* **💥 Part 1: Mega-Evolution Area Split:** Visual SVG area model blueprint that splits 2-digit multiplication into Tens + Ones boxes (e.g., $24 \times 7 = (20 \times 7) + (4 \times 7)$).

* **🧪 Part 2: Potion Fraction & Ratio Reactor:** Dual visual energy bars for comparing, adding, and converting fractions, decimals, and percentages.

* **🗺️ Part 3: Ozias & Marisa Gym Leader Story Quests:** Multi-step Pokémon adventure word problems tailored to the active Grade Dial, complete with a step-by-step blueprint detector.

### 🎮 Arena 4: Volt-Runner Arcade Mini-Game (`<canvas>`)

* Steer Ozias's active Partner across **3 Sky Lanes** using **Up/Down Arrow Keys**, **W/S**, or the on-screen touch lane buttons to fly through the gate displaying the correct solution to the live equation at the top of the screen!

### 📖 Arena 5: Living Pokédex (16 Creatures) & 5 League Gym Badges

* **16 Collectible Creatures:** Every creature defeated in the Battle Arena (or unlocked via **100-Coin Mystery Capsules**) joins Ozias's Living Pokédex with custom SVG artwork and spoken math lore.

* **5 Official Gym Badges:** Earn the **Boulder**, **Cascade**, **Thunder**, **Rainbow**, and **Master Dragon** badges to awaken **#016 Legendary Astralux**!

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Zero-Dependency Portability

* **100% Single-File HTML5 (`index.html`):** Self-contained HTML, CSS, SVG artwork, and Vanilla JavaScript—zero build steps or external assets required.

* **Self-Contained Local Sync & HTML Baker:** Includes the signature **Download Updated Game HTML** button (bakes `window.__OZIAS_POKEMATH_STATE__` directly into `index.html`), portable JSON export/import (`ozias_pokemath_sync.json`), clipboard sync codes, and browser `localStorage` persistence.

* **Web Audio API Synthesizer & Web Speech API:** Custom retro RPG attack sounds, Poké-Ball catch fanfare, and text-to-speech question reading with a one-click mute toggle.
